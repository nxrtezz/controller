SETTINGS

Configuration

Controller's operational settings are deliberately kept simple.

Data polling

Live vehicle positions are polled every 2 minutes for operators with active rules. Fleet and service data are refreshed every 10 minutes.

Notifications

Website alerts are kept in the activity log. Configure a Discord webhook on each rule to also post notifications to Discord.

Catalogue imports

Run python manage.py import_bustimes after deployment to import Bustimes vehicle types, liveries, operators, and services.

Account

User accounts and passwords are managed through the Django admin.
